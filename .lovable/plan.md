

# Enhance Talking Head Production Details with Hook Education

## Current State
The Production Checklist Panel already shows:
- Three hook types (Verbal, Written, Visual) in color-coded cards
- Line-by-line script with "Copy Script" button
- Text overlays with timing
- Delivery style tips
- Caption reminder

## What's Missing
The user wants **educational content** that helps creators understand:
1. **Why hooks matter** - general education on the three-hook system
2. **Why these SPECIFIC hooks were chosen** - based on `psychology_trigger` and `why_this_works` fields

## Solution

Enhance the expanded talking head section with two new educational components:

### 1. Three-Hook System Explainer (Collapsible)
A brief, always-visible intro section explaining the concept:

| Hook Type | Purpose | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Verbal | What you SAY first - stops the scroll | "—anyway, that's when I realized..." |
| Written | Text on screen - creates curiosity gap | "The moment everything changed" |
| Visual | What they SEE - sets the mood | Sitting in car, coffee in hand |

Include a one-liner: "These three hooks work together to capture attention in the first 3 seconds before viewers scroll past."

### 2. "Why This Works" Psychology Card
Display the `why_this_works` and `psychology_trigger` fields that are already being generated by the AI. This explains why *these specific* hooks were chosen for this creative.
